Spotting election misinformation
  • 1Check with the election office. Glitches and ballot shortages do happen — county officials usually confirm them quickly, along with how they were fixed.
  • 2Look for the candidate's own words. Claims about concessions and controversial quotes are easy to verify against a campaign's statements or the full video.
  • 3Read the actual court order. Supreme Court rulings are public — check the docket before trusting a post about what a justice did.
  • 4Question claims of tampered voting machines. Tabulators are tested and monitored, and real security problems get confirmed by election officials, not first revealed on social media.
  • 5Don't assume the absurd is fake. Some of the wildest stories this cycle were true. Verify before you dismiss, too.
