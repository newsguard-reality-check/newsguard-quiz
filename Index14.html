🔍 Don't just ask an AI chatbot

Grok can be a useful starting point, but like most AI chatbots, it struggles with accuracy — especially on breaking news. Here's what to do instead of taking its word for it:

  • 1 Trace it to the original source. Before trusting a claim, find out who posted it first and whether a credible outlet has independently reported it.
  • 2 Reverse-image and video search. Tools like Google Images or InVID can reveal whether a photo or clip is old, from a different event, or lifted from a video game or movie.
  • 3 Check multiple, established outlets. If something dramatic were true, more than one reputable news organization would be reporting it — not just a single viral post.
  • 4 Treat AI answers as a lead, not a verdict. Chatbots can be confidently wrong, and can even repeat rumors back as fact. Use their answers as a starting point for your own search, not the final word.
