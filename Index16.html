Spotting election misinformation
  • 1Go to the source on voting rules. State and county election offices publish the actual requirements — check there before trusting a viral post about what's legal at the polls.
  • 2Find the full clip. Out-of-context video and cropped quotes are a favorite tactic against candidates — track down the original speech or event.
  • 3Be wary of "official" flyers and documents. Fabricated campaign materials are easy to make and hard to trace — look for the campaign's own channels.
  • 4Check what the evidence actually shows. Claims of voter fraud often rest on documents or data that don't say what the post says they do.
  • 5Slow down when it confirms what you already think. Election claims built to enrage one side spread faster than they're checked.
