How to Spot a Fake Quote

1
Look for a primary source. A real quote should trace back to video, audio, or a wire-service transcript — not just a screenshot or a repost.
2
Watch for "too perfect" phrasing. Fabricated quotes often nail someone's tone but read like a caricature — every verbal tic packed into one line.
3
Check the specifics. Fake quotes tend to fall apart on a concrete detail — a date, a number, a name — that doesn't match the public record.
4
Search before you share. A quick search usually surfaces the original remarks — or confirms that nothing matches.
5
Consider the source. Accounts with a track record of spreading misinformation are more likely to be sharing a fabricated quote in the first place.
