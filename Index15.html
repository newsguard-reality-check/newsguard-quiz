Spotting misinformation about brands
  • 1Check the source. A screenshot with no link, byline, or dateline is a red flag — trace the claim back to where it supposedly originated.
  • 2Look for the company's own statement. Brands typically respond fast to viral claims — a quick search of the company's newsroom or verified social accounts often settles it.
  • 3Watch for "too on-the-nose" outrage. Claims designed to confirm what one side already believes about a brand spread faster than they're checked.
  • 4Reverse image search photos and video stills. Old or unrelated footage is one of the most common ways brand claims get faked.
  • 5Be skeptical of satire without a label. Some fabricated brand stories originate from satire sites and get stripped of context as they're shared.
